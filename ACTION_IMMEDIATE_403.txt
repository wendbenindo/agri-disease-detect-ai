VÉRIFIER LES POLITIQUES :
--------------------------
Dans SQL Editor, exécutez :

FROM pg_policies 
WHERE tablename = 'objects' 
AND policyname LIKE 'chat_images%';

Vous devez voir 4 lignes :
- chat_images_select_all (SELECT, public)
- chat_images_insert_auth (INSERT, authenticated)
- chat_images_update_auth (UPDATE, authenticated)
- chat_images_delete_auth (DELETE, authenticated)
